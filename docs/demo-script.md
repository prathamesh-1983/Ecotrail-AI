# 🎥 EcoTrail AI — 90-second demo script

## 0–10 sec — Problem

"Most AI experiences keep us looking at a screen. EcoTrail AI is designed to do the opposite: use local open-source AI to help us explore outside."

## 10–25 sec — Show the app

Show the home page, the Local AI badge, and the photo upload.

## 25–45 sec — AI demo

Upload a clear plant/nature photo.

Say:

"The image is sent to my local Ollama instance running Gemma 3 4B. There is no cloud AI API in this flow."

Show the result.

## 45–60 sec — Journal

Save the observation and open the journal.

## 60–75 sec — Outdoor test

Show the phone outdoors and a real observation.

## 75–90 sec — Why open source

"Using an open multimodal model makes the core AI inspectable and locally runnable. After downloading the model, the app can analyze photos without an internet connection."

Finish with:

"EcoTrail AI: explore more, scroll less."
